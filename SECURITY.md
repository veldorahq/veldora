---

## Reporting a Vulnerability

The Veldora team takes the security of our framework and its users seriously.

If you discover a security vulnerability within Veldora:

1. **Do not open a public issue or discussion.**
2. Send an email describing the vulnerability to **security@modrao.com** or **modraostudio@gmail.com**.
3. Please provide sufficient details to reproduce the issue, including proof of concept code if available.

We will acknowledge receipt of your vulnerability report within 48 hours and work with you on a timely resolution.
